When paintless repair is not the answer

Cracked or chipped paintThe panel will need paint work anyway.
Stretched metalVery deep or sharp impacts can stretch the steel beyond repair.
Sharp body lines and edgesSometimes possible, sometimes not. Photos tell.
Panels with old fillerPrevious body work under the paint limits what can be done.
